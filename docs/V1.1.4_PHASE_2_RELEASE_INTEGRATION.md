## 7. Distribution and source-licensing decisions

- The production manifest currently declares REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES, and the in-app updater performs a user-initiated self-update through Android Package Installer. Current Google Play policy says this permission may not be used for app self-updates except for device-management purposes. If Google Play distribution is intended, the current updater/permission needs a separate Play-compliant design or build variant; do not assume optional Device Owner mode automatically qualifies. See https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12085295 and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18265346.
- The repository is public and its LICENSE is Apache-2.0. R8 does not conceal the already-public source. Changing repository visibility now would not revoke public copies or rights already granted under the license. No visibility or license change was made.
- Owner decision required: confirm whether v1.1.4 is distributed only as a direct GitHub APK or is also intended for Google Play. The manifest permission was not changed pending this decision.

## 15. Resource-lifecycle review and latest exact-head CI

**Reviewed integration code head:** `788777cc157a9836b5f097b4426d4e56d98dd3ed`

### Resource-lifecycle corrections

- `RootCapabilityInspector`: the `su` child process now has a bounded wait, concurrent bounded stdout draining, stream closure, graceful termination followed by forced termination when needed, and bounded reader-thread joins. This prevents a full stdout pipe from blocking process completion and reduces the chance of leaving a diagnostic process/thread behind.
- `EvidenceLocationCapture`: one-shot location requests now return a cancellation handle. Cancellation removes the timeout callback, unregisters legacy location listeners, and cancels modern `CancellationSignal` requests without reporting a false "no fix" result.
- `SettingsActivity`: current-location capture is canceled in `onStop()`; late results are ignored unless the screen is resumed.
- `SettingsActivity` background executor: replaced the unbounded single-thread executor queue with one worker and an eight-task bounded queue. Queue saturation is surfaced to the owner rather than allowing unbounded queued work.
- `BluetoothCenterActivity` and `DeviceIntelligenceActivity`: their Activity-owned executors are shut down in `onDestroy()`; both stop their ranging query on stop/destruction.
- Camera evidence: bounded 15-second capture timeout; camera session, camera device and ImageReader are closed; callbacks are removed and the HandlerThread is asked to quit on completion.
- Ranging capability query: bounded 8-second timeout; timeout callbacks are removed and platform callbacks are unregistered.
- Alarm audio, tone, flash and sensor resources have explicit stop/release/unregister paths in their owning service/activity lifecycle.
- `LocalEvidenceVault` uses one daemon writer and a bounded 64-task queue. It is best-effort by design; saturation can discard queued diagnostic writes rather than grow memory without bound.

### Remaining resource caveats

- The Settings executor uses graceful shutdown so an already-running bounded backup/export operation can finish safely; at most eight queued operations remain when the Activity is destroyed. These tasks are finite and the queue is bounded, but they may briefly retain the Activity until the queue drains.
- The evidence writer's discard policy means an overloaded process can lose best-effort evidence metadata writes. This is a bounded-queue reliability trade-off, not an unbounded thread/queue leak.
- No physical-device memory profiling, repeated camera open/close soak test, or OEM background stress test was performed. Owner debug-app testing is still required.
